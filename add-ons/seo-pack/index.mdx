CoCart SEO Pack adds a product SEO endpoint that returns meta data and Schema.org structured data sourced from your active SEO plugin — Rank Math, SEOPress, The SEO Framework, All in One SEO, or Yoast SEO (last active plugin wins) — falling back to a built-in provider when none of those are active.

<Warning>
  CoCart SEO Pack is a separate plugin and requires its own license key. See [License Activation](/getting-started/license-activation) for setup.
</Warning>
