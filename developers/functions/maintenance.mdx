Plugin: <Badge color="blue">Community</Badge>

### `cocart_task_clear_carts()`

Plugin: <Badge color="blue">Community</Badge>

Clears all carts from the database and persistent carts from usermeta.

<Note>Made available since v3.1.2</Note>

**Parameters**

<ParamField query="return_results" type="bool">
  Return total of persistent carts cleared. Default: `false`.
</ParamField>

**Returns**: `int|void` — Number of saved carts or void.

- **Defined in**: [`includes/cocart-task-functions.php`](https://github.com/co-cart/co-cart/blob/trunk/includes/cocart-task-functions.php)
