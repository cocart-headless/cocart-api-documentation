<Warning>
    This API requires **CoCart Plus v2 or higher** to use.
</Warning>

<Info>
    This endpoint is intentionally public and requires no authentication, since the confirmation link is opened from an email client that may not share a session with the requester - possession of the emailed `key` is the proof of ownership. The link expires after 24 hours by default.
</Info>
